Pitch is a ball thrown
Pitch is throwing, roughly or casually
Pitch is sound
Pitch is the frequency, the speed, of vibration
Pitch is the incline, the climb
Pitch is the sway, the oscillation
Pitch is the measure of the rock
Pitch is a vigorous jogging motion, falling as another way of moving
Pitch is intensity, how much intensity, a level of intensity
Pitch is the distance between points and lines
Pitch is the distance between the teeth of a cogwheel, this distance between my teeth and your teeth
Pitch is tarmac
Pitch is asphalt
Pitch is semiliquid when hot
Pitch is sticky, sticks stuff together
Pitch is to smear, cover, coat
Pitch is viscous
Pitch is vicious
Pitch is slow
Pitch is the slowest substance
Pitch is an experiment of the slowest substance, dropping
Pitch is ongoing
Pitch is football
Pitch is an open field, a playing field
Pitch is a strip of ground between two stumps
Pitch is a proposal, a persuasion, a bid
Pitch is setting up camp, fixing yourself somewhere, some place
Pitch is to thrust into the ground, to stake
Pitch is to help or escalate, support or tackle
Pitch is to arrive, to turn up, to turn in.

-- Isabella Thorpe-Woods & Nora Raine Thompson
