Curatorial Programs

DRIFT

Drift is a monthly program presented by SUPR OMEN showcasing live-works by artists who push to the edge of the edge of their discipline.

Drift driving is a technique where the driver intentionally oversteers to cause rear-wheel traction loss, allowing the car to slide sideways through a corner while maintaining control and countersteering. Drift artists are presented with the opportunity to allow some creative traction loss to find the experimental limits of what live work can be.

The philosophy behind the program is that artists need to feel free to take risks in front of an audience for major breakthroughs to occur. This is a unique opportunity for the audience to be a part of a rare and vulnerable catalytic moment where creation occurs between the audience and the creator.

PITCH

Pitch is a monthly program presented by SUPR OMEN showcasing performances by early career dance & performance makers, curated by emerging dance & performance curators and makers.

Curators work solo or in teams to produce and present a season of three performances. Curators are encouraged to use the opportunity to work with emerging artists they would like to build long-term collaborative partnerships with and to develop their own curatorial approach to presenting dance. The philosophy behind the program is to create a way for emerging artists to support and invest in each other by building meaningful relationships in a field that often promotes competition over community.

The first season of Pitch is curated by Isabella Thorpe Woods and Nora Raine Thompson.

Curators are selected by nomination. If you would like to nominate a curator, please email info@supromen.com.

RINSE

Rinse is a quarterly work-in-progress event presented by SUPR OMEN highlighting artists of all disciplines in 10-minute showings of developing ideas. Inspired by cross-genre dialogue. Curated by C. Francis Fischer.

Featured artists include Jerry Leiblich, Matt Romein, Sonya Molansky, Natalie Raskin, E. Adamo, Jimin Seo, Lemuel E. Saputra & Noah Wertheimer.
