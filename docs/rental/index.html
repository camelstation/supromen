SUPR OMEN is a 1000 sf performance space adjoining 1000 sf of semi-private studios. We're located in Brooklyn at 1 Knickerbocker Ave on the second floor, two blocks from the Morgan L. Below is information about the performance space. To enquire about renting a studio, please contact info@supromen.com.

Rehearsal Memberships

Become a rehearsal space member for access to our 1000 sf rehearsal space, equipment, facilities, access to the calendar to make bookings directly and opportunities to share work in our quarterly member gatherings.

Half-membership $130/m for 10 hours
