DRIFT

Drift is a monthly program presented by SUPR OMEN showcasing live-works by artists who push to the edge of the edge of their discipline.

Drift driving is a technique where the driver intentionally oversteers to cause rear-wheel traction loss, allowing the car to slide sideways through a corner while maintaining control and countersteering. Drift artists are presented with the opportunity to allow some creative traction loss to find the experimental limits of what live work can be.

The philosophy behind the program is that artists need to feel free to take risks in front of an audience in order for major breakthroughs to occur. This is a unique opportunity for the audience to be a part of a rare and vulnerable catalytic moment where creation occurs between the audience and the creator.
