  <h2 class="shop-heading">DRIFT</h2>
  <p>Drift is a monthly program presented by SUPR OMEN showcasing live-works by artists who push to the edge of the edge of their discipline.</p>
  <p>Drift driving is a technique where the driver intentionally oversteers to cause rear-wheel traction loss, allowing the car to slide sideways through a corner while maintaining control and countersteering. Drift artists are presented with the opportunity to allow some creative traction loss to find the experimental limits of what live work can be.</p>
  <p>The philosophy behind the program is that artists need to feel free to take risks in front of an audience for major breakthroughs to occur. This is a unique opportunity for the audience to be a part of a rare and vulnerable catalytic moment where creation occurs between the audience and the creator.</p>
</div>

  <h2 class="shop-heading">PITCH</h2>
  <p>PITCH is a monthly program presented by SUPR OMEN showcasing performances by dance &amp; performance makers, curated by emerging dance &amp; performance curators and makers.</p>
  <p>PITCH curators work solo or in teams to produce and present a season of three performances. Curators are encouraged to use the opportunity to work with artists they would like to build long-term collaborative partnerships with and to develop their own curatorial approach to presenting dance. The philosophy behind the program is to create a way for emerging artists to support and invest in each other by building meaningful relationships in a field that often promotes competition over community.</p>
  <p>The first season of Pitch is curated by <a href="https://www.instagram.com/bellathorpewoods/" target="_blank" rel="noopener" style="text-decoration: underline;">Isabella Thorpe-Woods</a> and <a href="https://norarainethompson.org/about/" target="_blank" rel="noopener" style="text-decoration: underline;">Nora Raine Thompson</a>.</p>
  <p>Curators are selected by nomination. If you would like to nominate a curator, please email <a href="mailto:info@supromen.com" style="text-decoration: underline;">info@supromen.com</a>.</p>
</div>

  <h2 class="shop-heading">RINSE</h2>
  <p>Rinse is a quarterly work-in-progress event presented by SUPR OMEN highlighting artists of all disciplines in 10-minute showings of developing ideas. Inspired by cross-genre dialogue. Curated by C. Francis Fischer.</p>
  <p>Featured artists include Jerry Leiblich, Matt Romein, Sonya Molansky, Natalie Raskin, E. Adamo, Jimin Seo, Lemuel E. Saputra &amp; Noah Wertheimer.</p>
