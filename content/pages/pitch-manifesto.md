  <p>
    Pitch is a ball thrown<br>
    Pitch is throwing, roughly or casually<br>
    Pitch is sound<br>
    Pitch is the frequency, the speed, of vibration<br>
    Pitch is the incline, the climb<br>
    Pitch is the sway, the oscillation<br>
    Pitch is the measure of the rock<br>
    Pitch is a vigorous jogging motion, falling as another way of moving<br>
    Pitch is intensity, how much intensity, a level of intensity<br>
    Pitch is the distance between points and lines<br>
    Pitch is the distance between the teeth of a cogwheel, this distance between my teeth and your teeth<br>
    Pitch is tarmac<br>
    Pitch is asphalt<br>
    Pitch is semiliquid when hot<br>
    Pitch is sticky, sticks stuff together<br>
    Pitch is to smear, cover, coat<br>
    Pitch is viscous<br>
    Pitch is vicious<br>
    Pitch is slow<br>
    Pitch is the slowest substance<br>
    Pitch is an experiment of the slowest substance, dropping<br>
    Pitch is ongoing<br>
    Pitch is football<br>
    Pitch is an open field, a playing field<br>
    Pitch is a strip of ground between two stumps<br>
    Pitch is a proposal, a persuasion, a bid<br>
    Pitch is setting up camp, fixing yourself somewhere, some place<br>
    Pitch is to thrust into the ground, to stake<br>
    Pitch is to help or escalate, support or tackle<br>
    Pitch is to arrive, to turn up, to turn in.
  </p>
  <p>-- Isabella Thorpe-Woods &amp; Nora Raine Thompson</p>
